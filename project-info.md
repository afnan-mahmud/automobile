Project Overview
A fully customized, web-based business management software
built specifically for Dhaka Automobiles — covering every critical
area of operations from customer job management to employee
attendance, stock control, accounts, live job tracking, and more.
Everything in one system. Clean, simple, and built the way the
business actually works.

Software Features

Feature 1 — Customer Job Card and Invoice Management
When a vehicle arrives for servicing, a digital job card will be created
listing all the work that needs to be done. Based on that job card, an
invoice will be automatically generated and shared with the customer.
If any changes are needed — additional work added or items removed
— the invoice will be updated instantly and the revised version will be
ready to send. Every job, every bill, every update — tracked and
organized.

Feature 2 — Employee Management
Each employee will have their own profile within the system. Daily
attendance will be recorded and salary will be calculated
automatically based on actual hours worked. If an employee works
fewer hours than required, the deduction will be applied. If they work
overtime, the additional amount will be added. Employees will have
daily tasks assigned to them, and if a task is not completed on the
assigned day, it will automatically carry forward to the next day as a
pending task. Work reports for every employee will be available at any
time.

Feature 3 — Stock Management
Stock management will operate across two areas. First, when a
product is sold as a retail item, the quantity will be automatically
deducted from total stock. Second, when a vehicle is being serviced
and specific parts or products are used during the job, those items will
also be deducted from stock in real time. This ensures that the stock
figures are always accurate and up to date.

Feature 4 — Account Management
A complete picture of the business finances will be available at all
times. Daily service sales, cash received, bank deposits, mobile
banking transactions, total profit, outstanding dues, and all expenses
including product purchases and operational costs will be recorded
and trackable from a single dashboard.

Feature 5 — Live Job Tracking
For every vehicle under service, a live tracking view will show exactly how
many tasks have been assigned, which ones have been completed, and
what is still in progress. This tracking updates in real time based on
employee work reports. A shareable tracking link will be provided so the
customer can check the progress of their vehicle's service from anywhere,
at any time.

Feature 6 — Customer Reminder Messages
The admin panel will include a manual message sending feature, allowing
the team to send reminder messages to customers whenever needed —
whether for service follow-ups, due payments, or any other
communication.

Feature 7 — Warranty Card
A digital warranty card will be generated for relevant jobs. The card will
clearly show the start and end date of the warranty period, and specify
exactly which parts or services are covered under warranty. This gives the
customer clarity and protects the business from disputes.

Feature 8 — Discount Card
A discount card feature will allow specific customers to be assigned a fixed
discount percentage. The card will include details of the terms and
conditions under which the discount applies, ensuring that discounts are
given consistently and transparently.
